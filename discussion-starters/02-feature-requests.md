# 💡 What Feature Should MEDIA KOLFAT Add Next?

MEDIA KOLFAT 2.0.0 already offers guided COPY, advanced COPY/MOVE, duplicate detection, photo rotation, progress controls and local library organization. I'd love to hear which improvements would solve your biggest real-world problem.

Ideas to discuss (not promised features):

- Smarter organization and metadata workflows
- Better duplicate review and comparison
- More ways to browse large libraries
- Additional export/reporting options
- More accessibility or language support
- Optional advanced features for a future PRO edition

**Tell us three things:** (1) What are you trying to do? (2) How do you do it today? (3) What would success look like?

Please share one well-described idea per reply so others can discuss it. Suggestions help guide priorities, but they are **not commitments or release dates**.
