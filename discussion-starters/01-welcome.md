# 👋 Welcome to the MEDIA KOLFAT Community!

Welcome! This is a space for people who want to organize their photo and video collections without unnecessary complexity. **MEDIA KOLFAT 1.8.0** is available for Windows through the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9p0x091t7mv4?ocid=webpdpshare).

**ORDER FROM CHAOS.** 🖤

Introduce yourself if you like: What kind of photo or video collection are you managing? Is your biggest challenge finding files, organizing dates, dealing with duplicates, or working across several drives?

**Where to go:** Use the [Wiki](https://github.com/1n50mn14c933k/MEDIA_KOLFAT/wiki) for how-to guides, Discussions for questions and suggestions, and [Issues](https://github.com/1n50mn14c933k/MEDIA_KOLFAT/issues) for reproducible bugs. Security vulnerabilities should be reported **privately** using [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/1n50mn14c933k/MEDIA_KOLFAT/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Please be respectful. Don't post personal photos, private file paths, account details or credentials. This is a community for practical feedback and mutual help, not a place for unofficial installer downloads.
