# Library Overview and Activity History

MEDIA KOLFAT includes two ways to understand your library and recent work.

## Library Overview

Use **Library Overview** to inspect the available information about your collection, including media inventory, health, duplicates and organization-related summaries.

It is particularly useful when deciding which part of a large library to organize first. Treat summary information as a guide and confirm critical results against the files themselves.

## Activity History

**Activity History** provides a way to review completed scanning and organization activity.

Useful questions to answer:

- Which scan or organization activity happened recently?
- Did an operation complete or require attention?
- What should be investigated before running another large operation?

## Operation reports

COPY and MOVE operations also produce automatic reports. For an investigation, review the relevant report together with the Activity History entry and destination contents. Do not assume the history is a backup or a permanent forensic record.

If you are reporting a bug, share the version and **sanitized** details rather than raw personal file paths.
