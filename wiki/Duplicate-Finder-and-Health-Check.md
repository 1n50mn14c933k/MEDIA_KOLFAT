# Duplicate Finder and Photos Health Check

These tools help you understand a collection before deciding how to organize it.

## Duplicate Finder

The Duplicate Finder helps identify possible duplicate media. Review detected groups carefully:

- Verify that files really are duplicates, not separate exports or intentionally preserved versions.
- Check whether you are comparing originals, edits or different formats.
- Decide what to retain before taking any separate file-management action.
- Do not delete an only copy of an important image or video.

This wiki does not assume duplicate detection automatically deletes files.

## Photos Health Check

Photos Health Check provides information about media that may need your attention. It is a **check**, not a promise that damaged files will be repaired.

- Review the reported result.
- Check the original in an appropriate viewer when possible.
- Preserve copies before trying additional fixes in other software.
- If you report an issue, provide a sanitized description rather than attaching private media by default.

## Practical order of operations

1. Keep an independent backup.
2. Discover or browse the library.
3. Inspect duplicate and health findings.
4. Plan organization.
5. Organize with COPY first.
6. Verify the output and read the report.
