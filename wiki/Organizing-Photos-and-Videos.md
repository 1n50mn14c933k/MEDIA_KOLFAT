# Organizing Photos and Videos

MEDIA KOLFAT helps transform mixed photo and video folders into a more predictable collection, including **date-based organization** where usable date information is available.

## Plan your destination first

- Identify the source directories: camera exports, phone backups, external drives or network shares.
- Confirm the destination is the correct drive or folder and that sufficient free capacity exists.
- Keep an independent backup before moving original files.
- Avoid using a destination that could unexpectedly disconnect during processing.
- Check permissions on external and network drives before starting.

## A practical first-run workflow

1. Start in **Guided Mode**, preferably with a small sample folder.
2. Select the source and let MEDIA KOLFAT discover the files.
3. Check available dates and previews, particularly for old scans, edited files and media without useful EXIF metadata.
4. Choose an organization pattern appropriate for your collection.
5. Review the destination and expected result before starting.
6. Choose **COPY** to leave the originals in place; use Advanced **MOVE** only if you intend to transfer originals.
7. Observe the current-file and overall progress indicators. Version 1.8.0 also supports Pause, Resume and Cancel.
8. After completion, open both the organized destination and the operation report.
9. Spot-check photos and videos from different dates and folders.

## Dates and metadata

Cameras, phones and editors can store dates differently. When reliable capture metadata is not available, the app may use available fallback information; check the resulting structure before trusting a large batch.

## Duplicates and health checks

Use **Duplicate Finder** to inspect duplicate findings and **Photos Health Check** to identify potential media concerns. Do not assume every visually similar image is redundant or that a health scan repairs originals automatically.

## Safety checklist

- [ ] Source and destination paths reviewed
- [ ] Backup exists for important media
- [ ] Enough space and permissions verified
- [ ] COPY vs MOVE selected intentionally
- [ ] Progress and report reviewed
- [ ] Organized output spot-checked
