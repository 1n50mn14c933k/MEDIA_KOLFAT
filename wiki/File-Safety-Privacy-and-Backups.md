# File Safety, Privacy and Backups

**Your memories are worth protecting.** MEDIA KOLFAT is local-first software, but every file organization operation deserves care.

## COPY and MOVE: understand the consequences

**COPY:** Creates organized destination copies while aiming to keep source files in place. This is the recommended default, especially for beginners.

**MOVE:** Transfers originals to the selected destination, intentionally changing the original storage location. Available through Advanced Mode. Version 1.8.0 adds safeguards designed to reduce accidental loss, but verification and backups are still essential.

## Before using MOVE

1. Keep at least one **independent backup** on separate storage.
2. Confirm the selected source and destination are different and correct.
3. Confirm available destination capacity and reliable network/storage connectivity.
4. Start with a small test batch rather than your entire archive.
5. Review the in-app warning, preview and mode selection.
6. After processing, inspect representative results and read the generated MOVE report.
7. Do not remove backups just because an operation appears successful.

## Interrupted operations

MEDIA KOLFAT provides Pause, Resume and Cancel controls. If an operation is interrupted, use the status and report to determine what completed. Check both source and destination before restarting an operation or manually deleting anything. Avoid unplugging a drive or shutting down the computer during a write.

## Local processing and privacy

Normal organization workflows process your media on your Windows computer; they do not require uploading your photo or video library to KOLFAT servers. The [Privacy Policy](https://kolfat.app/privacy/) is authoritative for current data handling.

## Publishing logs or screenshots

Before asking for help, remove: real names, personal media, drive paths that identify you, account information, credentials and private network details. Never publish suspected vulnerabilities in public Discussions or Issues; see [private reporting guidance](https://github.com/1n50mn14c933k/MEDIA_KOLFAT/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

## If something looks wrong

Stop further changes, preserve original files and backups, and collect a **sanitized** operation report. Then use [[Troubleshooting and FAQ|Troubleshooting-and-FAQ]] or [official support](https://kolfat.app/support/).
